1 Folder

—
Every video in this folder (not its subfolders) is matched to its .srt, and the subtitle is aligned to the episode — to its embedded English subtitles or English audio when it has them (a dubbed audio track misleads the alignment), otherwise to its audio. The offset may change along the episode, e.g. at ad breaks. The synced subtitle replaces the original; the original is kept in an _original_subs subfolder.
Needs start_helper.bat running, plus ffsubsync (pip install ffsubsync) and ffmpeg (winget install Gyan.FFmpeg).
